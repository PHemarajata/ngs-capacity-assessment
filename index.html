Pathogen Genomic Surveillance Capacity Assessment

Three assessment forms for public health laboratory capacity for pathogen genomic surveillance. Each form is a single file that opens in any modern browser, saves answers on the device as you type and works offline once you save a copy.

Current forms

Before the visit
Short Form (v4.1)
Sent to each laboratory two to three weeks before the visit. One senior respondent completes it in about 30 minutes, and the answers define the scope of the visit.
On site, for each laboratory
Full Baseline (v8.1)
Completed on site with laboratory technical and quality staff, over one to two days for each laboratory.
On site, once for each site
Enabling Environment Annex (v1.1)
Completed with institutional leadership, and with national authorities where possible. Institutional and national questions are asked once for each site, not for every laboratory.

Current builds: Short Form v4.1 and Full Baseline v8.1, build 2026-11-12a. Enabling Environment Annex v1.1, build 2026-11-16a. Each form shows its build in the page footer.

To fill a form without internet, open it here first and save it to the device (in a desktop browser, File › Save Page As).
