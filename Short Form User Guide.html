Tips for a useful response

  • ✓
    Section 7 (Priorities) matters most. A one-sentence answer is not enough. Describe the gap, urgency, and what success looks like from your perspective.
  • ✓
    Best estimates beat blank fields. An order-of-magnitude guess ("~200 samples/year") is more useful than leaving it empty. Mark N/A only if truly not applicable.
  • ✓
    Multiple people can contribute. Share the file with relevant staff but have everyone work on the same device in the same browser to preserve all responses.
  • ✓
    Who to involve by section: Lab director for Sections 1, 2, 7 · Wet-lab staff for Sections 3 & 4 · Bioinformatician for Section 5 · Quality officer for Section 6.
  • ✓
    Section 2 (Scope) drives the full baseline. Mark everything you're doing, piloting, or seriously planning — even if early-stage.
